Pac-Man

Controls:
    Move: Arrow keys or WASD
    Pause: the game: P or ESC
    ESC/Space/Enter: Return to menu

Configuration:

Edit config.json to change:
    highscore_filename
    number of lives
    points_per_pacgum
    points_per_super_pacgum,
    points_per_ghost
    level_max_time
    maze sizes
